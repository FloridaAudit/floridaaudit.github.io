The monthly cost of a Florida home with a mortgage rose $313 after inflation from 2019 to 2024. Insurance was $67 of it.

Average monthly cost for owners with a mortgage, in 2024 dollars. Insurance is 10% of the bill and 22% of the rise. Census caps reported insurance at $10,000 a year and 2.9% of owners are at the cap, so that step is a floor.
