Who Governs

Which party has held Florida's governorship, House and Senate, and since when?

Who decides
Voters elect the governor and all 160 legislators. Since 1999 one party has held all three, and with them state law and the budget.
What these charts establish
Who held each office in each year, from official election and legislative records. It does not by itself show what that control caused.
  • Florida has elected a Republican governor and Legislature in every election since 1998.
  • Republicans have held the Florida House since 1997.
  • Republicans have held the Florida Senate since 1995.
