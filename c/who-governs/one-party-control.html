Who Governs

Florida has elected a Republican governor and Legislature in every election since 1998.

Governor and seats by party, as elected, Florida House (120) and Senate (40), 1980 to 2026. Charlie Crist, elected governor as a Republican in 2006, left the party in 2010 to run for the U.S. Senate as an independent.
