Amendment 3 would reduce local non-school property tax revenue by an estimated $11.83 billion a year by fiscal 2031-32.

Estimated reduction in local non-school property tax revenue by the government that levies it. On the November 3, 2026 ballot; needs 60% to pass. Ten counties carry $7.54B of it, led by Miami-Dade $1.39B and Broward $1.19B, with all 67 in the CSV. It keeps growing after the exemption stops stepping up in 2028, because the conference counts the sixth year as the recurring impact and the lower assessment cap compounds. Holds 2025 tax rates constant. Nominal dollars.
