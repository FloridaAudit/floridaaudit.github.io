The yearly assessment cap on rental and commercial property would fall from 10% to 5%.

Maximum annual growth in taxable assessed value for non-homestead, non-agricultural parcels, non-school levies. The current 10% cap has been in place since 2008. The chart is an illustration calculated here, not a forecast: one parcel assessed at $1,000,000 in 2026 whose market value rises 12% a year, the condition under which a cap binds.
