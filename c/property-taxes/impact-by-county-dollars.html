Property Taxes

Ten counties carry $7.54 billion of the $11.83 billion, and two thirds of it comes off county government.

Recurring annual reduction in local non-school property tax revenue once the exemption is fully phased in, for the ten largest of Florida's 67 counties, split between the county government and the cities and special districts inside it. Duval has no county-government bar: Jacksonville and Duval County are one consolidated government, which the table files as a municipality, so its whole reduction sits in the purple bar. All 67 counties are in the CSV behind this chart. Holds 2025 tax rates constant. Nominal dollars.
