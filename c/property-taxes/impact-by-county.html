Property Taxes

As a share of today's budget, eight counties will lose more than half of their non-school property tax.

The same reduction as the dollar chart, measured as a share instead, with the dollars after each name so the scale stays visible. Both charts count every local government in the county, the county itself plus its cities and special districts, against every non-school levy raised there: recurring annual reduction estimated for FY2031-32, against each county's 2025 non-school levies, calculated here. The two figures are years apart on purpose: the reduction is measured against what a county raises today, and the levy base will itself grow by 2031, so the share of that later base is smaller than the share shown here. Holds 2025 tax rates constant.
