Amendment 3 would raise the non-school homestead exemption from $51,411 to $250,000 by 2028.

Maximum exempt assessed value for a qualifying homestead, non-school levies. Proposed amounts apply to people who were Florida residents by December 31, 2026, and adjust for inflation from 2029. Nominal dollars.
