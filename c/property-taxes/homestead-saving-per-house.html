Property Taxes

From 2028, the exemption would cut a homestead's non-school property tax by at most $2,075 a year.

Yearly saving for one qualifying homestead, not for a household: the exempt value added over the 2026 maximum ($98,589 in 2027, $198,589 in 2028), taxed at an average non-school rate, with no rate or inflation changes. $2,075 is the ceiling, reached only by a home with enough assessed value to use the whole exemption; below that a home saves in proportion to its assessed value, and a renter saves nothing. The statewide figure is calculated here from Department of Revenue millage. Pinellas County is the sole county who published its own illustration, at 12.2011 mills against the state's 10.449.
