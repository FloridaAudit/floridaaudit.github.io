Property Taxes

At Pinellas County's 2025 average rate, the full exemption would cut a homestead's non-school taxes by $2,423 in 2028.

Published illustration: additional exempt value over the 2026 maximum ($98,589 in 2027, $198,589 in 2028) at the 2025 countywide average non-school rate of 12.2011 mills, with no rate or inflation changes. Homes with less assessed value save less.
