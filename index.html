How to check any number

Who Governs

  • Florida has elected a Republican governor and Legislature in every election since 1998.
  • Republicans have held the Florida House since 1997.
  • Republicans have held the Florida Senate since 1995.

Property Taxes

  • By 2028 the maximum non-school homestead exemption would be $250,000 under Amendment 3, against $52,805 under current law.
  • From 2028, the exemption would cut a homestead's non-school property tax by at most $2,075 a year.
  • Florida's 2.92 million renter households would get no homestead exemption.
  • Amendment 3 would reduce local non-school property tax revenue by an estimated $11.83 billion a year by 2031.
  • Amendment 3 lists seven permitted uses for county and city property taxes.
  • Amendment 3 is on the ballot because the Legislature put it there: 75-26 in the House, 30-9 in the Senate.
  • As a share of today's budget, eight counties will lose more than half of their non-school property tax.
  • Ten counties carry $7.54 billion of the $11.83 billion, and two thirds of it comes off county government.
  • Separately, the yearly assessment cap on rental and commercial property would fall from 10% to 5%.
