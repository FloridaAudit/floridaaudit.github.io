Who Governs

  • Republicans have held Florida's governorship, House and Senate since 1999.

  • Republicans have held the Florida Senate since 1995.

Property Taxes

  • Amendment 3 would raise the non-school homestead exemption from $51,411 to $250,000 by 2028.
  • At Pinellas County's 2025 average rate, the full exemption would cut a homestead's non-school taxes by $2,423 in 2028.
  • Renters get no homestead exemption. Rental and commercial property would get a 5% annual assessment cap instead of 10%.
  • Amendment 3 would reduce local non-school property tax revenue by an estimated $11.83 billion a year by 2031.
  • Amendment 3 lists seven permitted uses for county and city property taxes.
  • The Legislature put Amendment 3 on the ballot by votes of 75-26 in the House and 30-9 in the Senate on June 2, 2026.
